What it is

GlassHood is AI monitoring with dashboards, for business and IT alike. It connects to a running integration pipeline or other complex system — today a solution on Google Cloud, read from Azure — and shows where the whole system and each of its components stand, live. Rules and anomaly detection raise alerts from the measurements, and every 3 hours an AI review reads each component's logs and raises its own alarms, labelled as found by the AI review. A short AI overview says in plain words how the whole system is doing and what needs attention, and each component has its own. Business owners and IT teams look at the same dashboard, and nothing they do there changes the monitored system.

The problem it solves

With current tools, business owners cannot see where their system stands, end to end. An integration pipeline runs through many systems, and each is watched by its own team with its own tool, in its own technical terms. When a connection stops or a message no longer arrives, the first sign is often a wrong result further down the chain, and the business owner learns of it late and second-hand. GlassHood puts the whole chain in one view that business and IT read together, down to each component, and points at the part where it breaks.

How it works

AI reads the logs of every component

GlassHood reads the measurements and logs of every component in an integration pipeline or other complex system, and only reads: it installs nothing in the systems it watches, and toward them every call is list, get or describe, made with a short-lived, viewer-only token and no stored keys. Threshold rules and anomaly detection check the measurements first, and AI runs on top: every 3 hours it reviews each component's logs and raises its own alarms, labelled as found by the AI review. An AI alarm must quote the log lines it rests on, or it is dropped. A signed-in operator can ask the AI to investigate one alarm deeper and get a report; that investigation may search the web for the error.

A dashboard shows where the system and each component stand

A live map shows every component and every connection with its health, refreshed every 10 minutes, and a short AI overview says how the whole system and each component are doing and what needs attention. Each component's logs are one click away. Business and IT see the same dashboard, and nothing they do there changes the monitored system.

Alerts to the right people, and tickets in your ticketing system

Every alert carries a severity, critical, warning or info, and goes to the distribution lists set for that severity. For each critical or warning alert, GlassHood opens a ticket in your ticketing system, for example an incident in ServiceNow, with its urgency and impact set from the severity. A critical AI alarm stays until a signed-in person acknowledges it.

Regular updates, as agreed

GlassHood sends regular updates as agreed: the system's status, the open tickets and who is working on them, with links.
