What it is

GlassHood is AI monitoring with dashboards, for business and IT alike. It connects to a running integration pipeline or other complex system and shows where the whole system and each of its components stand, live. Rules and anomaly detection raise alerts from the measurements, and every 3 hours an AI review reads each component's logs and raises its own alarms, labelled as found by the AI review. A short AI overview says in plain words how the whole system is doing and what needs attention, and each component has its own. Business owners and IT teams look at the same dashboard, and nothing they do there changes the monitored system.

The pain it solves

With current tools, business owners cannot see where their system stands, end to end. An integration pipeline runs through many systems, and each is watched by its own team with its own tool, in its own technical terms. When a connection stops or a message no longer arrives, the first sign is often a wrong result further down the chain, and the business owner learns of it late and second-hand. GlassHood puts the whole chain in one view that business and IT read together, down to each component, and points at the part where it breaks.

How it works

  • AI reads the logs of every component in an integration pipeline or other complex system.
  • A dashboard shows where the system and each component stand, with direct access to every log.
  • Alerts the right distribution lists by severity and opens tickets in your ticketing system (e.g. ServiceNow).
  • Sends regular updates as agreed: system status, open tickets and who is working on them, with links.
